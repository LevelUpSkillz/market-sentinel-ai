# Canadian and Québec commercialization review

The current scope is owner-only research and simulated execution. Before selling signals, individualized copy recommendations, discretionary decisions or execution, obtain qualified Canadian/Québec advice about the actual business, instruments and contracts. A disclaimer or AI label does not determine adviser/dealer registration status.

Primary sources reviewed: AMF/CSA Staff Notice 21-329 at https://lautorite.qc.ca/fileadmin/lautorite/reglementation/valeurs-mobilieres/0-avis-acvm-staff/2021/2021mars29-21-329-avis-acvm-en.pdf ; CIRO guidance at https://www.ciro.ca/firms/becoming-crypto-asset-trading-platform ; current CSA platform status at https://www.securities-administrators.ca/crypto-platforms-regulation-and-enforcement-actions/crypto-platforms-authorized-to-do-business-with-canadians/ ; FCAC risks/registration guidance at https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/payment/digital-currency.html .

Review the adviser/dealer business trigger, securities and derivatives classification, provincial registration, suitability/KYC, conflicts, advertising and performance claims. Also review Québec private-sector privacy/Law 25, data minimization for linkable public-wallet information, consumer and French-language requirements, and AML/FINTRAC applicability if later handling transfers/exchanges. These are engineering launch gates, not a legal opinion that any particular license is or is not required.

No commercial signal product or live execution is enabled. Backtests, paper returns and observed wallet profits must not be marketed as proven net follower profitability.
